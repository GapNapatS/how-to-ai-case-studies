# How to AI: three real conversations

These are word-for-word transcripts (redacted) of three conversations between Gap and his AI, from the How to AI class, Session 3. Read them on your phone or iPad. Keep them as a copy of how Gap talks to his AI, and borrow whatever helps your own work.

| Case | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Case 1: "Did you really watch it?"](case-1-did-you-really-watch-it.md) | The AI says it watched a video. It hadn't. How asking, testing and checking fixed it. |
| [Case 2: "No, no, no, no"](case-2-no-no-no-no.md) | Building a class timer by talking. Gap corrects the AI, approves a design table, and the AI says what it did not test. |
| [Case 3: "Checking by looking"](case-3-checking-by-looking.md) | Two AI mistakes (a misdescribed room, two deleted clocks) that only show up because Gap looks. |

Each file starts with what the conversation is, what to look for, and how to read it. Names, links and personal details are hidden in `[brackets]`.
